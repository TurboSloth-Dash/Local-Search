# Gulf South Forgotten History - v6

A free, iPad-friendly research workbench for community historians and regional researchers.

## What changed in v6

The app was simplified around seven sections:

1. Search
2. Explore
3. Case
4. Timeline
5. Evidence
6. Reports
7. Advanced

The technical search machinery is mostly hidden.

## Major research upgrades

- Multi-newspaper Library of Congress search across selected Gulf South titles.
- Subject detection: person, business/organization, or place.
- Historical spelling, abbreviation, and OCR-aware variants.
- Historical-concept search terms for openings, closures, moves, deaths, property, brands and contracts.
- Business and organization entity recognition.
- Address and exact-record-identifier extraction.
- Progressive clue following.
- Duplicate / repeated-result suppression.
- New-information detection.
- Negative-search logging to avoid repeating dead ends.
- Research-gap detection.
- Business-specific source plans.
- Explore-a-place discovery mode.
- Evidence review with simple confidence labels.
- Professional PDF and dynamic Excel outputs.
- Technical controls moved to Advanced.

## Important rule

Automatic means the app actually searched it. Open & search means it is a useful free source that may require its own website or interface.
